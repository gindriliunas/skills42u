About This Service

First aid training for Rochester's business community

Rochester is the historic heart of Medway — a cathedral city with one of the largest concentrations of commercial, industrial, and public-sector employers in north Kent. The city's economy spans professional services and retail in the High Street and Rochester Retail Park, significant engineering and manufacturing heritage around the Medway industrial estates and Rochester Airport, a major NHS footprint centred on Medway Maritime Hospital in nearby Gillingham, and a substantial education sector. The HSE's First Aid Regulations require every Rochester employer to carry out a needs assessment and provide adequate first aid provision — the right qualification depends on your specific industry and workforce size.

Skills 42U comes to your Rochester or Medway premises — whether that's a Rochester city centre office, a Chatham industrial unit, or a Strood warehouse — with every piece of training equipment included. CPR manikins, AED trainers, trauma dressings, and all practical resources are brought to you. Certificates are issued on the day of training and your business is audit-ready from the moment the course ends.

We also cover first aid training across Medway, first aid courses in Gravesend, and first aid training in Sittingbourne — useful if your business operates from multiple sites across north Kent.
