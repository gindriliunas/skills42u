Who Needs Paediatric First Aid in Essex?

Paediatric First Aid for Essex nurseries, childminders & early years providers

Essex has one of the largest early years sectors in the South East — with more than 2,000 Ofsted-registered settings ranging from solo childminders in Brentwood and Rayleigh to large day nursery chains in Chelmsford, Colchester and Southend-on-Sea. For every one of these settings, the EYFS statutory framework imposes the same non-negotiable requirement: at least one person holding a current, Ofqual-regulated Paediatric First Aid certificate must be present at all times when children are on the premises or on outings. This is not a recommendation — it is a statutory duty verified at every Ofsted inspection.

Skills 42U delivers the full 2-day Paediatric First Aid course on-site at your Essex setting. Whether you are a registered childminder operating from a home in Basildon, a pre-school attached to a primary school in Braintree, or a nursery chain with multiple sites across Chelmsford and Harlow, we travel to you. We bring infant and child manikins, AED trainers and all practical equipment — your staff train in the environment they actually work in, with no travel disruption or supply cover costs.

Note that Paediatric First Aid is a separate qualification from the standard employer-obligation Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) course. Most Essex early years settings need both: PFA to meet the EYFS requirement and EFAW (or the full First Aid at Work) to satisfy the HSE's duty on employers under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981. We can advise on the most time-efficient and cost-effective way to train your team for both — call 07481 344486 for a no-obligation conversation.

Course Content

What the 2-day Paediatric First Aid course covers

The full Ofqual-regulated syllabus — everything your Essex childcare staff need to respond confidently to a paediatric emergency.

👶 Day 1 — Infant & Child Life Support

  • Role and responsibilities of the paediatric first aider
  • Managing an unresponsive infant or child
  • Infant CPR — compressions & rescue breaths
  • Child CPR — compressions & rescue breaths
  • AED use on children (paediatric electrode pads)
  • Recovery position for infants & children
  • Choking — infant, child and adult techniques

🔧 Day 2 — Emergency Scenarios & Medical Conditions

  • Anaphylaxis & EpiPen / Jext administration
  • Febrile seizures & epilepsy management
  • Severe bleeding, wounds & shock
  • Burns & scalds in children
  • Fractures, dislocations & head injuries
  • Meningitis awareness & non-blanching rash
  • Diabetes hypoglycaemia in children

Assessment: continuous practical observation across both days plus a short written knowledge check. Ofqual-regulated certificates issued the same day to all delegates who meet the pass standard.
