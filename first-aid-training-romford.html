What Romford Employers Need to Know

First aid compliance for Romford businesses — logistics, retail, care and hospitality

Romford's logistics and warehousing businesses — particularly those operating from sites along the A12 and Rom Valley Way — face higher first aid requirements than their retail counterparts. Forklift operations, racking systems, loading bays and the physical demands of distribution work place these sites in a moderate-to-high hazard bracket under HSE guidance, which typically means the 3-day First Aid at Work qualification rather than EFAW alone. The same applies to any manufacturing or engineering operation on Romford's trading estates. If your site involves heavy machinery, chemical storage or manual handling of heavy goods, carry out an HSE needs assessment before defaulting to EFAW — the exposure to serious injury is the determining factor, not the number of staff.

The Havering care sector — care homes, supported living and domiciliary care providers across Hornchurch and Upminster — operates under dual oversight from both the CQC and the HSE. CQC inspection reports routinely note first aid certificate gaps, and the HSE classifies care home environments as higher hazard due to manual handling risks and the potential for serious incidents involving vulnerable residents. If you run a care setting in Romford or Hornchurch, the Emergency First Aid at Work course is a minimum — many care homes opt for the 3-day FAW to ensure comprehensive coverage.

Romford's childcare sector serves a substantial family population across the RM postcodes and bordering Dagenham and Chadwell Heath. Nurseries, preschools, out-of-school clubs and registered childminders must have at least one person on the premises at all times holding a current Paediatric First Aid certificate whenever children are present — an Ofsted requirement that has no exemptions. Certificates must be Ofqual-regulated and renewed every 3 years. We deliver at your setting with no need for staff to travel.
