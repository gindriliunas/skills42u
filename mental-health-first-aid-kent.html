Why Mental Health First Aid?

Mental health is your most common workplace health risk

Mental health conditions are now the leading cause of workplace absence in the UK. The HSE's 2023/24 statistics recorded 17.1 million working days lost to work-related stress, anxiety, and depression — more than musculoskeletal disorders, more than physical injury, and more than any other category. Yet most workplaces have extensive provision for physical first aid and almost none for mental health crises.

Mental Health First Aid (MHFA) training closes that gap. A trained Mental Health First Aider is not a therapist or counsellor — they are a first point of contact: someone qualified to recognise the early signs of mental ill health, to have a supportive, non-judgemental conversation, and to guide a colleague towards the right professional or organisational support. Exactly as a physical first aider keeps someone stable until the ambulance arrives, an MHFA helps someone through a crisis until professional help takes over.

What Your Team Will Learn

What the 2-day Mental Health First Aider course covers

The full 2-day MHFA England course qualifies delegates as accredited Mental Health First Aiders. The curriculum covers:

  • The mental health continuum — understanding the spectrum from wellbeing to crisis
  • Depression — signs, symptoms, and how to have a safe conversation with someone showing signs
  • Anxiety disorders — generalised anxiety, panic attacks, phobias, OCD, PTSD
  • Psychosis — recognising the signs and responding safely
  • Eating disorders — awareness and safe first response
  • Suicide and self-harm — Safe Messaging guidelines, how to ask directly about suicidal thoughts, and what to do in an acute crisis
  • Substance misuse — recognising when substance use signals an underlying mental health issue
  • The MHFA Action Plan — a practical five-step framework for any mental health first aid situation
  • Stigma and discrimination — how to challenge it at work and build a mentally healthy culture
  • Signposting — local Kent and national resources, Employee Assistance Programmes, and NHS pathways

Delegates complete the course with a 3-year MHFA England certificate, a comprehensive manual, and a pocket reference card. The course is delivered at your Kent premises and can be split across two consecutive days or two separate dates, depending on your team's availability. FAQs

Common questions about Mental Health First Aid in Kent

Mental Health First Aid is an internationally recognised training programme that teaches employees to recognise the signs of mental health conditions, provide initial non-clinical support, and guide colleagues towards appropriate professional help. A Mental Health First Aider is a first point of contact — not a therapist — equivalent in principle to a physical first aider but for mental health.
There is currently no UK law that mandates MHFA specifically, but the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 require employers to assess and manage workplace stress risks. The HSE's guidance strongly recommends MHFA as a practical measure. Many large contractors and public-sector clients now require MHFA provision as part of their supplier requirements.
Physical first aid covers bodily injury and medical emergencies. Mental health first aid addresses psychological crises and early signs of mental ill health. The two are complementary — many Kent employers book both EFAW and MHFA training in the same period to ensure comprehensive first response cover across their whole team.
MHFA England recommends one Mental Health First Aider per 50 employees as a starting point. Many organisations in high-stress sectors — healthcare, education, construction — aim for a higher ratio. At minimum, every workplace should have at least one MHFA alongside physical first aiders.
