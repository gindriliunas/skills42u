About This Service

First aid training for Dover's business community

Dover is one of the UK's most strategically significant towns — home to the busiest passenger ferry port in Europe, a substantial logistics and freight sector, growing construction activity, and a town centre that ranges from retail and hospitality to professional services. Each of these industries carries its own first aid risk profile, and the HSE's First Aid Regulations require every employer to ensure adequate first aid cover matched to those risks.

Port-adjacent operations, freight forwarding yards, construction sites along the A2 corridor, and logistics hubs in Whitfield typically require the full 3-day First Aid at Work qualification. Town centre retail, hospitality, and office businesses are generally well-served by the 1-day Emergency First Aid at Work course. If you're not sure which applies to your Dover business, call us on 07481 344486 — we'll advise in five minutes at no charge.

Skills 42U comes to your Dover premises with every piece of training equipment included. Manikins, AED trainers, dressings, and all practical resources travel with our instructor. Certificates are issued on the day, and your business is audit-ready from the moment training ends. We also cover first aid training in Folkestone, Ashford, and Canterbury — useful if your business operates across multiple east Kent sites.
