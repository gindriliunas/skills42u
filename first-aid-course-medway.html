About This Service

First aid training built around Medway businesses

Medway is one of Kent's largest and most diverse business areas — from the logistics and manufacturing firms around Medway City Estate and Chatham Maritime, to the retail and hospitality businesses along Chatham High Street, to the education and healthcare providers throughout Rochester and Gillingham. Whatever your sector, the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981 require you to have adequate first aid provision for your workforce.

Skills 42U makes compliance straightforward. We come to your Medway premises — whether that's a warehouse on the Medway City Estate, an office in Rochester, or a nursery in Gillingham — with all equipment included, and your team leaves with accredited certificates the same day. No travel, no venue hire, no disruption to your operations.

We also deliver first aid courses in Maidstone, first aid training in Dartford, and across the wider Kent area — so if you have sites in multiple locations, we can coordinate training across all of them.
