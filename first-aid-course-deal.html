About This Service

First aid training for Deal's business community

Deal is a historic coastal market town in Dover District with a strong independent retail, hospitality, and tourism economy along its seafront and High Street. The town's care sector — including residential homes and domiciliary care providers serving an older local population — makes Paediatric and standard first aid training a consistent requirement. Deal Castle and the surrounding heritage sites also attract tourism-related businesses whose staff benefit from recognised first aid training.

Skills 42U comes to your Deal premises — conference room, back office, or any suitable space — with every piece of training equipment included. Manikins, AED trainers, dressings, and all practical resources are brought to you. Certificates are issued on the day of training, and your business is audit-ready immediately.

We also cover first aid training in Dover, first aid courses in Folkestone, and first aid training in Margate — useful if your business operates across multiple east Kent locations.
