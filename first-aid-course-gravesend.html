About This Service

First aid training for Gravesend's business community

Gravesend is one of north Kent's most commercially active towns, with a business mix that spans retail and professional services in the town centre, significant logistics and industrial operations in Northfleet and around Ebbsfleet Garden City, and a growing construction sector as development expands along the Thames Estuary corridor. The HSE's First Aid Regulations require every employer to provide adequate first aid cover — the specific qualification needed depends on your industry and workforce size.

Skills 42U comes to your Gravesend or Northfleet premises — conference room, site welfare cabin, or any suitable space — with every piece of training equipment included. Manikins, AED trainers, dressings, and all practical resources are brought to you. Certificates are issued on the day of training, and your business stays audit-ready from the moment the course ends.

We also cover first aid training in Dartford, first aid courses in Medway, and across north and west Kent — useful if your business operates from multiple sites.
