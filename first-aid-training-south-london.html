First Aid Training Across South London

On-site first aid courses for South London employers — NHS, construction, retail, hospitality and childcare

South London employs hundreds of thousands of people across ten diverse boroughs, each with its own industry concentration and first aid obligations under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981. The borough of Southwark alone is home to major NHS facilities at Guy's Hospital and King's College Hospital Denmark Hill, the Borough Market and Bermondsey hospitality corridor, and some of London's most active construction activity — including the ongoing Elephant & Castle town centre regeneration. For the NHS estates teams, facilities management contractors and hospitality businesses operating in this part of South London, the Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) certificate is the HSE-approved baseline for low-to-medium hazard environments, while contractors on live construction or complex building services projects require the full 3-day First Aid at Work qualification. The HSE is explicit that a first aid certificate is a qualification, not a substitute for a written needs assessment — employers must still determine appropriate provision for their specific premises and workforce.

To the east, the London Borough of Greenwich covers a wide range of employers: Queen Elizabeth Hospital Woolwich, the Greenwich Peninsula mixed-use development, a significant logistics and light-industrial base at Charlton and Erith, and active retail at Woolwich Arsenal and Eltham town centres. The peninsula regeneration has brought hundreds of construction workers and safety-critical operatives to SE10 — all requiring current first aid certification to CDM 2015 standards. Skills 42U delivers First Aid at Work on-site at construction welfare facilities and project offices, and Emergency First Aid at Work at commercial premises throughout Greenwich, Bexley, Lewisham and Woolwich. We work around shift patterns and programme milestones so training never disrupts operational continuity.

The third major demand driver across South London is the early-years sector. Lambeth, Lewisham and Southwark together contain some of London's highest concentrations of registered childcare providers, nurseries and childminder networks. Ofsted requires that every EYFS childcare setting has designated staff holding a valid, Ofqual-regulated Paediatric First Aid certificate — without which the setting cannot be registered or re-registered. Skills 42U delivers the full 2-day Paediatric First Aid course at nurseries and preschools throughout all South London boroughs, bringing all infant manikins, AED trainers and equipment, and scheduling around each setting's daily routine to cause minimum disruption.

South London Industries We Train

First aid training built around how South London businesses actually operate

South London's employer base is far more varied than its residential reputation suggests. The area south of the Thames is home to major NHS trusts, some of the UK's most active regeneration projects, a thriving retail and hospitality sector, and thousands of registered childcare settings. Each sector carries different first aid obligations — and Skills 42U has delivered training across all of them.

NHS & Healthcare: King's College Hospital at Denmark Hill, Guy's Hospital at London Bridge, Lewisham Hospital and Queen Elizabeth Hospital Woolwich are among South London's largest single-site employers. NHS estates teams, facilities management contractors and patient transport operators require current first aid certification — typically the full 3-day First Aid at Work qualification for roles involving manual handling, clinical adjacency or building services. Skills 42U is experienced with NHS estates scheduling and can deliver around ward patterns and on-call rotations. See our guide to first aid requirements for care settings.

Construction & Regeneration: South London is home to some of the UK's most significant regeneration projects — Elephant & Castle (over 10,000 new homes and a rebuilt town centre), Battersea Power Station (one of Europe's largest mixed-use schemes), Kidbrooke Village, the Greenwich Peninsula, and continuing Crossrail 2 enabling works. Under CDM 2015, first aid provision must be in place from day one of any notifiable project, and all construction sites require the full First Aid at Work qualification for designated first aiders. Skills 42U delivers FAW training at site welfare facilities across South London, working around shift patterns and programme windows. Read our guide to first aid requirements on construction sites.

Retail & Hospitality: The Whitgift Centre and Centrale in Croydon, Bromley High Street and the Lewisham Shopping Centre are South London's major retail hubs, each employing hundreds of staff across multiple units. The Borough Market and Bermondsey Beer Mile, Brixton Market and the East Dulwich village strip represent South London's vibrant independent hospitality sector — where EFAW training for kitchen and front-of-house teams is both a legal obligation and a practical necessity in high-footfall public environments. Croydon businesses and Bromley retailers already train on-site with Skills 42U. See also: first aid requirements for retail businesses.

Early Years & Childcare: Lambeth, Lewisham and Southwark together have some of London's highest concentrations of registered childcare providers. Ofsted will not register or re-register any EYFS setting where designated staff do not hold a valid Ofqual-regulated Paediatric First Aid certificate. Skills 42U delivers the full 2-day Paediatric First Aid course at nurseries, preschools and childminder networks across all South London boroughs, scheduling sessions to minimise disruption to daily childcare provision. Across Sutton and the broader South London area, we work with settings ranging from single-room childminder homes to nurseries with 80+ children on roll. Read our guide: first aid requirements for schools and early years settings.
