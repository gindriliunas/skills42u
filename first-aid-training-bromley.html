What Bromley Employers Need to Know

First aid requirements for businesses in the London Borough of Bromley

Under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981, every UK employer must carry out a first aid needs assessment and provide adequate cover for their staff. The HSE determines the minimum requirement based on workforce size and the nature of the hazards involved. For the majority of Bromley's office and retail businesses — whether in the town centre near The Glades, along Orpington High Street, or in Beckenham's commercial strip — at least one trained EFAW first aider on site at all times is the standard expectation.

Bromley has a significant and growing health and social care sector. Princess Royal University Hospital in Orpington, along with numerous residential care homes, nursing homes and supported-living providers across the borough, falls into the HSE's higher-hazard classification. Staff in these settings are typically required to hold the full 3-day FAW certificate. The CQC expects documentary evidence of current first aid training at every inspection. For a breakdown of how this applies to your specific team size, read our guide on how many first aiders your business needs.

The borough also has a large childcare sector — nurseries, preschools and registered childminders in Bromley, Beckenham, Penge and Orpington must ensure at least one member of staff holds a current Paediatric First Aid certificate whenever children are in their care. These certificates expire after 3 years. Ofsted expects them to be renewed before they lapse — see our guide to first aid certificate renewal deadlines for how to stay ahead of expiry and what to do if an inspection is approaching.

Construction activity has also increased significantly in Bromley, with regeneration projects around the town centre and new residential developments across Orpington and Petts Wood. Construction sites carry a higher HSE hazard rating, and site managers should review whether EFAW or the full FAW is required for their specific workforce. Our guide to first aid on construction sites covers the relevant regulations in full.
