What Croydon Employers Need to Know

First aid at work requirements for Croydon businesses

Under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981, every UK employer must carry out a first aid needs assessment and ensure adequate first aid cover for their workforce. The HSE bases the minimum requirement on two factors: workforce size and the level of hazard your business presents. For most Croydon offices and retail businesses — whether in the Croydon Opportunity Area, along London Road, or in the business parks around Purley Way — at least one trained EFAW first aider on site at all times is the standard expectation.

Croydon's extensive healthcare and social care sector adds further requirements. The borough is home to Croydon University Hospital and numerous care homes, nursing homes, supported-living providers and GP practices. The HSE places these settings in a higher hazard classification, meaning designated first aiders are typically required to hold the full 3-day FAW certificate rather than the EFAW. The CQC expects evidence of current, valid first aid training during inspections. For a full breakdown by sector and team size, read our guide on how many first aiders your business needs.

Croydon also has a large and diverse childcare sector. The borough's nurseries, preschools, childminders and out-of-school clubs registered with Ofsted must have at least one current Paediatric First Aid certificate holder present whenever children are in their care. These certificates expire after 3 years — check our guide to first aid certificate renewal for how to plan renewals in advance and what to do if an Ofsted visit is imminent.
