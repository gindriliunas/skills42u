About This Service

First aid training for Gillingham's business community

Gillingham is the largest of the five Medway towns by population and one of the most commercially diverse. The town spans a wide range of business types: retail and professional services concentrated around Gillingham High Street and the Pentagon Shopping Centre, significant manufacturing and light industrial activity at Gillingham Business Park and along the Medway City Estate, and a large public sector employer base anchored by the Medway NHS Foundation Trust at Medway Maritime Hospital and multiple educational settings. The HSE's First Aid Regulations require every employer to provide adequate first aid cover — the specific qualification needed depends on your industry and workforce size.

Skills 42U comes to your Gillingham premises — whether that's an office on the High Street, a unit on the Business Park, a school in Rainham, or a care home in Twydall — with every piece of training equipment included. Manikins, AED trainers, dressings, and all practical resources are brought to you. Certificates are issued on the day of training.

We also cover first aid training across Medway, first aid courses in Rochester, and first aid courses in Sittingbourne — useful if your business operates from multiple sites in north Kent.
