About This Course

Paediatric First Aid for Kent nurseries, schools & childminders

The Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) statutory framework requires that at least one person holding a current Paediatric First Aid certificate must be present at all times when children are on your premises or on outings. For Ofsted-registered childminders, nurseries, pre-schools, and other early years settings, this is a non-negotiable compliance requirement — not a recommendation.

Kent has over 1,500 registered early years settings — from childminders operating in Maidstone and Medway to large nursery chains in Canterbury and Dartford. Skills 42U delivers the full 2-day Paediatric First Aid course on-site at your setting, anywhere across the county. We bring infant and child manikins, AED trainers, and all practical equipment. Your team trains in the environment they work in, with all the flexibility that comes from not having to travel to an off-site venue.

Paediatric First Aid is a separate qualification from the standard workplace Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) course. Most early years settings need both — PFA to meet the EYFS requirement, and EFAW or FAW to meet the HSE employer obligation for staff. We can advise on the most efficient training plan for your setting; call us on 07481 344486 for a free conversation.

Course Content

What the 2-day Paediatric First Aid course covers

The full Ofqual-regulated syllabus — everything your staff need to respond confidently to a paediatric emergency.

👶 Day 1 — Infant & Child Life Support

  • Role of the paediatric first aider
  • Managing an unresponsive infant or child
  • Infant CPR — compressions & rescue breaths
  • Child CPR — compressions & rescue breaths
  • AED use on children (paediatric pads)
  • Recovery position for infants & children
  • Choking — infant, child, and adult techniques

🔧 Day 2 — Emergency Scenarios

  • Anaphylaxis & EpiPen administration
  • Febrile seizures & epilepsy
  • Bleeding, wounds & shock
  • Burns & scalds in children
  • Fractures & head injuries
  • Meningitis awareness & rash recognition
  • Diabetes emergencies in children

Assessment: continuous practical observation throughout both days + short written knowledge check. Certificates issued same day to all delegates who meet the pass standard.
