About This Service

First aid training for Swanley's business community

Swanley is a busy market town in west Kent sitting at the junction of the M25 and A20 — one of the most commercially active road corridors in the South East. That position makes it a natural location for logistics, distribution, and construction businesses, as well as a range of retail and professional services in the town centre and surrounding villages. The HSE's First Aid Regulations require every employer to carry out a first aid needs assessment and provide adequate first aid cover — and the industries concentrated around Swanley typically have above-average first aid obligations given the physical nature of the work.

Skills 42U comes to your Swanley premises — whether that's a logistics unit near Junction 3 of the M25, a construction site, or a town centre business — with every piece of training equipment included. Manikins, AED trainers, dressings, and all practical resources are brought to your site. Certificates are issued on the day training is completed, and your business is audit-ready immediately.

We also cover first aid training in Dartford and Sevenoaks — the two towns that bracket Swanley — which is useful if your business operates across multiple west Kent sites.
