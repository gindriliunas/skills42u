About This Service

First aid training built around Sittingbourne businesses

Sittingbourne sits at the heart of Swale — north Kent's industrial and logistics corridor between Medway and Faversham. The Eurolink Industrial Estate is home to hundreds of businesses spanning manufacturing, food production, warehousing, and distribution. These higher-hazard workplaces are exactly where the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981 demand rigorous first aid provision: not just an EFAW-qualified first aider, but in many cases the full 3-day First Aid at Work qualification.

Skills 42U delivers all three accredited courses directly to your Sittingbourne premises. Whether you run a distribution depot, a food processing unit, an office in the town centre, or a nursery serving local families, we assess your needs and recommend the right qualification. All equipment is included, certificates are issued the same day, and your team trains in the environment they actually work in — making the skills stick.

We also cover neighbouring areas — first aid training in Medway, first aid courses in Maidstone, and first aid training in Ashford — useful for businesses operating across multiple Kent sites.
