What the EFAW Course Covers

Everything your Essex team learns on the 1-day Emergency First Aid at Work course

The Emergency First Aid at Work qualification is the HSE's recommended minimum for lower-hazard workplaces. In a single training day delivered at your Essex premises, your nominated first aiders will be able to manage the emergency situations most likely to occur in an office, shop, restaurant, or small business environment. The Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981 require every UK employer to provide adequate first aid cover — for most Essex office and retail businesses, the EFAW meets that requirement entirely.

❤️ CPR & AED Use

Adult CPR to Resuscitation Council UK guidelines, plus hands-on practice with an AED trainer. Delegates leave knowing exactly how to use the defibrillator in your workplace.

💨 Unconscious Casualty

Safe management of an unconscious breathing person, including the recovery position, airway maintenance, and monitoring while awaiting emergency services.

🥵 Choking

Recognition and management of mild and severe choking in adults, including back blows and abdominal thrusts. A common workplace emergency in catering and warehouse settings.

🩸 Bleeding Control

Managing minor and severe bleeding, including direct pressure, wound dressing, and recognition of shock. Delegates practice bandaging techniques on real training dressings.

🔥 Burns & Scalds

First aid for thermal burns including cooling protocol, wound covering, and when to call 999. Particularly relevant for hospitality and catering businesses along the Southend and Colchester high streets.

🫣 Medical Emergencies

Recognition and initial management of heart attack, stroke, diabetic emergency, seizure, and anaphylaxis — the medical events most likely to occur in any workplace regardless of sector.

Is EFAW Right for Your Essex Business?

Who the 1-day EFAW qualification is designed for

The Emergency First Aid at Work course is the right choice for Essex businesses operating in lower-hazard environments — where the primary risks are slips, trips, minor cuts, medical emergencies, and the occasional burn rather than machinery contact, working at height, or hazardous substance exposure. The HSE's first aid guidance is clear: the level of training required must match the results of your first aid needs assessment.

EFAW is typically right for:

  • Offices & professional services
  • Retail & shops
  • Restaurants & hospitality
  • Schools & nurseries (admin staff)
  • Small businesses (under 25 staff)
  • Estate agents & letting offices
  • Hair & beauty salons
  • Gyms & leisure centres

FAW is required when:

  • Warehousing & distribution
  • Construction & trades
  • Manufacturing or factory work
  • Working at height or with plant
  • Chemical or hazardous substance use
  • Large workforce (50+ employees)
  • Multiple shift patterns
  • Remote or lone working environments

Essex has one of the largest logistics and distribution footprints in the South East, anchored by DP World London Gateway at Thurrock and the warehousing corridor running along the A13 and M25. Businesses operating in this environment almost always sit above the EFAW threshold and need the full First Aid at Work qualification for their operational teams. Many Essex employers with a mixed workforce — an office team alongside a warehouse floor — run EFAW for admin staff and FAW for operational staff in the same booking to cover both risk profiles in one visit.

FAQs

Common questions about EFAW in Essex

Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) is a 1-day HSE-approved first aid qualification regulated by Ofqual at Level 2. It covers the core emergency skills every workplace first aider needs: CPR, use of an AED, management of an unconscious casualty, choking, bleeding control, burns treatment, and recognition of common medical emergencies. The certificate is valid for 3 years and is accepted by the HSE, Ofsted, Essex County Council and most UK employers. EFAW is designed for lower-hazard workplaces — offices, retail, hospitality, and small businesses where the risk profile does not demand the fuller 3-day First Aid at Work course.
EFAW is appropriate for lower-hazard workplaces such as offices, shops, small hospitality businesses, and school administrative staff. The full 3-day First Aid at Work (FAW) is required where the HSE identifies higher hazards — this covers a large share of Essex's economy, including the warehousing and distribution operations around Thurrock and DP World London Gateway, manufacturing sites in Basildon and Braintree, and construction firms working the A12/M25 corridor. If you are unsure which applies to your Essex business, call us on 07481 344486 and we will advise you in under 5 minutes.
The HSE guidance for lower-hazard workplaces suggests: fewer than 25 employees — consider at least an appointed person or one EFAW-trained first aider; 25 to 50 employees — at least one EFAW first aider; more than 50 employees — at least one first aider per 100 workers. These are starting points, not ceilings. Essex businesses with lone workers, multiple floors, split shifts, or high visitor footfall should factor those situations into their needs assessment.
