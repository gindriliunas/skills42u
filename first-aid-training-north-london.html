First Aid Training Across North London

On-site first aid courses for North London employers — NHS, construction, education, retail and logistics

North London's five boroughs represent one of the most varied employment landscapes in the UK, spanning world-class research hospitals, some of the country's largest active infrastructure projects, major university campuses, and a significant industrial and logistics corridor stretching from the North Circular into Enfield. Under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981, every employer in this area must carry out a written first aid needs assessment and ensure that adequate first aid provision — including designated trained first aiders — is in place for all working hours. The appropriate level of training depends on the specific hazard profile of each workplace, not simply the size of the business.

The borough of Camden illustrates this range clearly. At one end, UCL and University College London Hospitals (UCLH) on Euston Road form one of the UK's largest academic health science centres — with thousands of NHS estates staff, facilities management contractors and clinical support workers requiring current first aid certification, usually the full 3-day First Aid at Work qualification for higher-risk roles. A kilometre to the north, the King's Cross HS2 enabling works represent one of the most active construction zones in Britain, where the CDM 2015 regulations require first aid provision from the first day of any notifiable project — with all designated first aiders on-site holding the full First Aid at Work (FAW) certificate. At the other end, the independent hospitality businesses of Camden Market, the offices around Fitzrovia, and the creative agencies clustered between Bloomsbury and King's Cross require the 6-hour Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) certificate as their HSE-compliant baseline. Skills 42U delivers all three course levels directly at premises throughout Camden — with no travel surcharge and no minimum group fee above what's stated.

In Haringey, the picture is anchored by three specific demand drivers. The Tottenham Hotspur Stadium — one of Europe's most technically advanced 62,000-capacity venues — employs hundreds of event safety, ground operations and stadium maintenance staff whose first aid obligations span CDM site works and live-event HSE compliance simultaneously. The Wood Green town centre, currently undergoing substantial regeneration, brings active construction alongside a concentrated retail and food service sector, all with distinct first aid obligations. And the Tottenham Hale regeneration corridor — one of the Mayor of London's priority Growth Areas — is generating significant volumes of construction, logistics and residential management employment, each with its own first aid training requirements. For businesses across Haringey's varied employment base, Skills 42U can schedule EFAW, FAW and Paediatric First Aid courses around shift patterns, event programmes and site access windows.

The third major area of first aid demand across North London is the early-years and childcare sector. Camden, Islington and Haringey together contain extremely high concentrations of registered childcare settings relative to their populations — a function of the density of working-age families with young children and the relatively high female employment rates in North London's service and knowledge economy. Ofsted requires all EYFS-registered settings to have designated staff holding a valid, Ofqual-regulated Paediatric First Aid (PFA) certificate, without which the setting cannot be registered or re-registered. Skills 42U delivers the full 2-day Paediatric First Aid course at nurseries, preschools and childminder networks throughout all five North London boroughs, scheduling sessions during school days to cause minimum disruption to daily childcare provision. Read more about first aid requirements for schools and early years settings.

North London Industries We Train

First aid training built around how North London businesses actually operate

North London's employer base is defined by four major sectors, each with distinct first aid obligations and scheduling constraints. Skills 42U has trained businesses across all of them.

NHS & Healthcare: University College London Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust — covering UCLH on Euston Road, the Royal National ENT Hospital, Eastman Dental Hospital and the Heart Hospital — is one of North London's largest single employers. The Royal Free London NHS Foundation Trust operates across the Royal Free Hampstead, Barnet and Chase Farm hospitals, while the Whittington Hospital serves Archway, Islington and the surrounding N-postcodes. North Middlesex University Hospital in Edmonton is the primary acute site for Enfield and parts of Haringey. NHS estates contractors, building services teams, patient transport operations and clinical adjacency staff across all these sites require up-to-date first aid certification — typically the 3-day First Aid at Work qualification for manual-handling and plant-maintenance roles. See our guidance on first aid requirements in care settings.

Construction & Infrastructure: HS2's Euston and King's Cross enabling works are among the most significant active construction programmes in Britain, generating demand for First Aid at Work-qualified personnel across principal contractors, sub-contractors and specialist suppliers. Beyond HS2, the Brent Cross Cricklewood regeneration scheme — a £4bn mixed-use development straddling the Barnet and Brent boundary — remains one of London's largest active development sites. Tottenham Hale's emerging residential quarter, the Alexandra Palace estate works and ongoing school rebuild programmes across Enfield and Barnet all generate consistent demand for CDM-compliant first aid training delivered on-site at welfare facilities and project offices.

Logistics & Warehousing: The Lee Valley and Brimsdown industrial corridor in Enfield is one of the most significant concentrations of logistics and light-industrial employment in Greater London. Businesses operating from Brimsdown Business Park, the Innova Park, Enfield Island Village and the Meridian Water development area — including major e-commerce fulfilment operations — are subject to the full weight of HSE manual-handling and first aid regulations. Warehouse and distribution environments of this type typically require the 3-day First Aid at Work qualification rather than EFAW, given the hazard profile of order-picking, vehicle movements and heavy goods handling. Skills 42U delivers on-site at warehouse facilities throughout Enfield, working around shift patterns and logistics-window constraints.

Education & Early Years: UCL, City St George's University of London, London Metropolitan University and Middlesex University together employ thousands of academic, professional services and estates staff across North London campuses. The early-years sector is equally significant — Camden, Islington and Haringey each have large registered childcare populations, and Ofsted will not register or re-register any EYFS setting without valid Paediatric First Aid certificates for designated staff. Skills 42U schedules PFA training to fit around daily nursery routines and term-time patterns across all North London boroughs. Read our guide to first aid requirements for office and education environments. We also offer Mental Health First Aid training for employers in education and high-pressure professional settings.
