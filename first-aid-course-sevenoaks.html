About This Service

First aid training built around Sevenoaks businesses

Sevenoaks is one of Kent's most prosperous business districts — a commuter town on the A21 corridor with a diverse mix of professional services firms, financial advisers, estate agents, independent retailers, and hospitality businesses serving the town centre and surrounding villages. Schools and nurseries — including Sevenoaks School itself — represent a significant employer base with specific first aid obligations. Whatever your sector, the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981 require you to have adequate first aid provision in place.

Skills 42U makes compliance simple. We travel to your Sevenoaks premises with all training equipment included — no venue hire, no staff travel time, no disruption. Your team trains in their own workplace, making the learning directly relevant to their environment. Accredited certificates are issued the same day, valid for three years.

We also deliver first aid courses in Tunbridge Wells, first aid training in Dartford, and across Maidstone and the wider Kent area — useful if your business has multiple sites.
