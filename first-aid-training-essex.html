What Essex Employers Need to Know

First aid at work requirements for Essex businesses

Every employer in the UK — regardless of business size or sector — is required by law to conduct a first aid needs assessment and ensure adequate first aid provision. In practice, the HSE's first aid guidance points to two key variables: the size of your workforce and the hazard level of your workplace.

For most Essex office and retail businesses — a small team in Chelmsford or a shop on Southend High Street — a single trained EFAW first aider is sufficient. For Essex's large logistics and distribution employers operating at London Gateway, the Thurrock industrial estates, or Harlow's business parks, the higher hazard profile means the full 3-day FAW certificate is required and the HSE may also recommend multiple trained first aiders based on your workforce size. See our guide on how many first aiders your business needs for a full breakdown by employee count.

Essex childcare settings — private nurseries, childminders, and preschools registered with Ofsted — face a separate requirement: at least one member of staff with a current Paediatric First Aid certificate must be present at all times when children are in the setting. Certificates expire after 3 years. Our guide to first aid certificate renewal explains what happens when certificates lapse and how to plan for renewal before your Ofsted visit.
