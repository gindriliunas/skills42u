What the EFAW Course Covers

Everything your Kent team learns on the 1-day Emergency First Aid at Work course

The Emergency First Aid at Work qualification is the HSE's recommended minimum for lower-hazard workplaces. In a single training day delivered at your Kent premises, your nominated first aiders will be able to manage the emergency situations most likely to occur in an office, shop, restaurant, or small business environment. The Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981 require every UK employer to provide adequate first aid cover — for most office and retail businesses in Kent, the EFAW meets that requirement entirely.

❤️ CPR & AED Use

Adult CPR to Resuscitation Council UK guidelines, plus hands-on practice with an AED trainer. Delegates leave knowing exactly how to use the defibrillator in your workplace.

💨 Unconscious Casualty

Safe management of an unconscious breathing person, including the recovery position, airway maintenance, and monitoring while awaiting emergency services.

🥵 Choking

Recognition and management of mild and severe choking in adults, including back blows and abdominal thrusts. A common workplace emergency in catering and care settings.

🩸 Bleeding Control

Managing minor and severe bleeding, including direct pressure, wound dressing, and recognition of shock. Delegates practice bandaging techniques on real training dressings.

🔥 Burns & Scalds

First aid for thermal burns including cooling protocol, wound covering, and when to call 999. Particularly relevant for hospitality, catering, and laboratory environments.

🫣 Medical Emergencies

Recognition and initial management of heart attack, stroke, diabetic emergency, seizure, and anaphylaxis — the medical events most likely to occur in any workplace regardless of sector.

Is EFAW Right for Your Kent Business?

Who the 1-day EFAW qualification is designed for

The Emergency First Aid at Work course is the right choice for Kent businesses operating in lower-hazard environments — where the primary risks are slips, trips, minor cuts, medical emergencies, and the occasional burn rather than machinery contact, working at height, or hazardous substance exposure. The HSE's first aid guidance is clear: the level of training required must match the results of your first aid needs assessment.

EFAW is typically right for:

  • Offices & professional services
  • Retail & shops
  • Restaurants & hospitality
  • Schools & nurseries (admin staff)
  • Small businesses (under 25 staff)
  • Estate agents & letting offices
  • Hair & beauty salons
  • Gyms & leisure centres

FAW is required when:

  • Manufacturing or factory work
  • Construction & trades
  • Warehousing & logistics
  • Working at height or with plant
  • Chemical or hazardous substance use
  • Large workforce (50+ employees)
  • Multiple shift patterns
  • Remote or lone working environments

Many Kent businesses have mixed workplaces — an office team alongside a warehouse or production floor. In these cases, we often recommend EFAW for the office and administrative staff and the full First Aid at Work qualification for the operational teams. If you are in the manufacturing sector, our dedicated manufacturing first aid training page covers the specific requirements for Kent production environments in detail.

FAQs

Common questions about EFAW in Kent

Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) is a 1-day HSE-approved first aid qualification regulated by Ofqual at Level 2. It covers the core emergency skills every workplace first aider needs: CPR, AED use, management of an unconscious casualty, choking, bleeding control, burns treatment, and recognition of common medical emergencies. The certificate is valid for 3 years and accepted by the HSE, Ofsted, local authorities, and UK employers across all sectors. EFAW is designed for lower-hazard workplaces — offices, retail, hospitality, and small businesses where the risk profile does not demand the fuller 3-day First Aid at Work course.
EFAW is appropriate for lower-hazard workplaces such as offices, shops, small hospitality businesses, and school administrative staff. The full 3-day First Aid at Work (FAW) is required where the HSE identifies higher hazards — manufacturing, construction, logistics, and environments involving machinery or hazardous materials. The correct level is determined by a first aid needs assessment. If you are unsure which applies to your Kent business, call us on 07481 344486 and we will advise you in under 5 minutes at no cost.
The HSE guidance for lower-hazard workplaces suggests: fewer than 25 employees — consider at least one appointed person or one EFAW-trained first aider; 25 to 50 employees — at least one EFAW first aider; more than 50 employees — at least one first aider per 100 workers. Businesses with lone workers, multiple floors, high visitor footfall, or split-site operations should factor those situations into their needs assessment. Having more than the minimum is never a problem — gaps in cover are.
