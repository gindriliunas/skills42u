About This Service

First aid training for Dartford's business community

Dartford sits at the gateway to Kent on the M25/A2 corridor — one of the UK's busiest logistics and commercial zones. The area around Ebbsfleet, Bluewater, and Stone is home to distribution centres, construction firms, and retail parks where workplace injury risk is above average. Under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981, businesses in high-risk environments must have a trained first aider on-site — not just an appointed person.

Skills 42U travels to your Dartford, Swanley, Gravesend, or Ebbsfleet premises — no venue hire required, all equipment provided. Your team trains at their workplace and certificates are issued the same day.

We also serve businesses in Medway and first aid training in Maidstone — useful if you have sites across the north Kent corridor.
