About This Service

First aid training for Faversham's business community

Faversham is one of mid-Kent's most distinctive market towns, with a business base that spans retail and professional services in the historic town centre, food and drink manufacturing — including the brewery and associated industries — logistics and warehousing around the Creek and industrial estates, and a growing residential population that sustains childcare and education providers. The HSE's First Aid Regulations require every employer to provide adequate first aid provision — the right qualification depends on your industry and workforce size.

Skills 42U comes to your Faversham premises — office, meeting room, production floor, or any suitable space — with every piece of training equipment included. Manikins, AED trainers, dressings, and all practical resources are brought to you. Certificates are issued on the day of training, and your business remains audit-ready from the moment the course ends.

We also cover first aid training in Sittingbourne, first aid courses in Canterbury, and across the Swale and mid-Kent corridor — useful if your business operates from multiple sites.
