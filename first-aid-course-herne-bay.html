About This Service

First aid training for Herne Bay's business community

Herne Bay is a thriving coastal town in the Canterbury District, with a business economy shaped heavily by tourism and hospitality — hotels, B&Bs, holiday parks, restaurants, and cafes see significant seasonal staffing increases each summer. The town also has a growing care sector, a number of primary schools and nurseries, and an active retail high street. The HSE's First Aid Regulations apply equally to every employer in Herne Bay regardless of size — from a five-person cafe to a care home with 60 staff.

Herne Bay businesses face specific considerations that urban employers don't. Ambulance response times along the north Kent coast can be longer than in Canterbury city centre or Medway — which is one reason the HSE's needs assessment guidance points towards having more trained first aiders in locations where emergency services take longer to arrive. It's a practical reason why on-site trained provision matters more, not less, for coastal and semi-rural employers.

Skills 42U travels to your Herne Bay premises with all training equipment included. We also cover neighbouring Whitstable and Canterbury — ideal if your business operates across multiple sites along the coast or inland.
