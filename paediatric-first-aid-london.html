Who Needs Paediatric First Aid in London?

Paediatric First Aid for London nurseries, schools & childminders

London has the largest concentration of registered early years providers of any UK region — more than 6,000 nurseries, pre-schools and childminder settings spread across all 32 boroughs and the City. For every one of these settings, from a converted townhouse nursery in Islington to a large corporate-sponsored nursery serving Canary Wharf employees, the EYFS statutory framework imposes the same non-negotiable requirement: at least one person holding a current, Ofqual-regulated Paediatric First Aid certificate must be present at all times when children are on the premises or on an outing. Ofsted inspectors verify this at every London inspection, and it is one of the most common compliance gaps flagged in inspection reports.

Skills 42U delivers the full 2-day Paediatric First Aid course on-site at your London setting. Whether you run a registered childminding business from a flat in Hackney, manage a pre-school attached to a primary school in Barnet, or oversee a multi-site nursery chain spanning Croydon and Sutton, we travel to you. We bring infant and child manikins, AED trainers and all practical equipment — your team trains in the room they actually work in, with no travel disruption, no supply cover costs and no need to close early for training.

Paediatric First Aid is a separate qualification from the standard employer-obligation Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) course. Most London nurseries and schools need both: PFA to meet the EYFS requirement for staff working directly with children, and EFAW or the full First Aid at Work to satisfy the HSE's duty on employers under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981 for the wider workforce. We can advise on the most time-efficient and cost-effective way to cover your whole team, whether your setting is in Westminster, Ealing, Redbridge or anywhere in between. Course Content

What the 2-day Paediatric First Aid course covers

The full Ofqual-regulated syllabus — everything your London childcare staff need to respond confidently to a paediatric emergency.

👶 Day 1 — Infant & Child Life Support

  • Role and responsibilities of the paediatric first aider
  • Managing an unresponsive infant or child
  • Infant CPR — compressions & rescue breaths
  • Child CPR — compressions & rescue breaths
  • AED use on children (paediatric electrode pads)
  • Recovery position for infants & children
  • Choking — infant, child and adult techniques

🔧 Day 2 — Emergency Scenarios & Medical Conditions

  • Anaphylaxis & EpiPen / Jext administration
  • Febrile seizures & epilepsy management
  • Severe bleeding, wounds & shock
  • Burns & scalds in children
  • Fractures, dislocations & head injuries
  • Meningitis awareness & non-blanching rash
  • Diabetes hypoglycaemia in children

Assessment: continuous practical observation across both days plus a short written knowledge check. Ofqual-regulated certificates issued the same day to all delegates who meet the pass standard.
