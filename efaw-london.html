Why London Businesses Choose EFAW

The HSE baseline for the vast majority of London workplaces

London's economy is overwhelmingly office and service-based, and that shapes first aid obligations differently than in a manufacturing or logistics county. Under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981, every employer must provide adequate first aid cover — and for the finance, legal, tech, media and retail businesses that dominate the City, Canary Wharf and the West End, the Emergency First Aid at Work qualification meets that requirement in a single day, without the cost or disruption of the full 3-day course. We deliver EFAW directly at London premises, so your team never has to travel to a public course venue or lose a half-day of productivity.

Multi-tenant office towers present a specific challenge: a single floor of 30–50 staff often shares a building with several other companies, a shared reception, and lift lobbies with heavy footfall. We regularly advise London tenants on how many EFAW-trained first aiders their floor plate actually needs once visitor traffic, floor count and evacuation routes are factored in — not just the headline employee number.

❤️ CPR & AED Use

Adult CPR to Resuscitation Council UK guidelines, plus hands-on AED practice — relevant given the growing number of defibrillators installed in London office lobbies and retail units.

💨 Unconscious Casualty

Safe management of an unconscious breathing person, recovery position and airway maintenance while awaiting the London Ambulance Service.

🥵 Choking

Recognition and management of mild and severe choking — a frequent scenario in London's dense hospitality and catering sector.

🩸 Bleeding Control

Direct pressure, wound dressing and shock recognition, practised hands-on with real training dressings.

🔥 Burns & Scalds

Cooling protocol, wound covering and 999 escalation criteria — particularly relevant for London's hospitality and catering workforce.

🫣 Medical Emergencies

Recognition of heart attack, stroke, diabetic emergency, seizure and anaphylaxis — the events most likely in any office or public-facing London workplace.

Is EFAW Right for Your London Business?

Who the 1-day EFAW qualification is designed for

EFAW is the right choice for lower-hazard environments — the primary risks are slips, trips, minor cuts, medical emergencies and the occasional burn, not machinery, working at height or hazardous substances. The HSE's first aid guidance is clear that the level of training must match your written needs assessment, not a default assumption.

EFAW is typically right for:

  • City & Canary Wharf offices
  • Retail & West End shops
  • Restaurants & hospitality venues
  • Tech & creative studios
  • Estate agents & letting offices
  • Hair & beauty salons
  • Gyms & leisure centres
  • Small businesses (under 25 staff)

FAW is required when:

  • Construction & site-based trades
  • Warehousing & logistics depots
  • Working at height or with plant
  • Facilities & maintenance teams
  • Chemical or hazardous substance use
  • Large workforce (50+ employees)
  • Multiple shift patterns
  • Remote or lone working roles

Many London businesses have a mixed workforce — a City office team alongside a facilities or maintenance crew, or a retail head office with warehousing attached. In these cases we typically recommend EFAW for the office-based majority and the full First Aid at Work qualification for the higher-hazard teams. Common questions about EFAW in London

Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW) is a 1-day HSE-approved first aid qualification regulated by Ofqual at Level 2. It covers CPR, AED use, management of an unconscious casualty, choking, bleeding control, burns treatment and recognition of common medical emergencies. The certificate is valid for 3 years and is accepted by the HSE, Ofsted, the Care Quality Commission and every London borough council. EFAW is designed for lower-hazard workplaces — offices, retail, hospitality and small businesses — which describes the majority of employers across Central and Greater London.
For most London office environments — professional services, finance, tech, media and retail head offices — EFAW meets the HSE's requirement in full. The full 3-day First Aid at Work (FAW) course is required where the HSE identifies higher hazards: construction sites, warehousing, logistics depots, and workplaces involving machinery or working at height.
