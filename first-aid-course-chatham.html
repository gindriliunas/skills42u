About This Service

First aid training for Chatham's business community

Chatham is the commercial and administrative heart of Medway, with one of the most varied business economies in north Kent. The Pentagon Shopping Centre and Chatham High Street are home to hundreds of retail and hospitality businesses. Chatham Maritime Enterprise Zone houses a growing cluster of technology, media, and professional services employers. The historic Dockyard area and surrounding business parks attract engineering, logistics, and maritime industry operations — many of which carry the elevated workplace risks that require the full HSE First Aid at Work qualification rather than the 1-day EFAW.

Skills 42U comes to your Chatham premises — conference room, site welfare cabin, or any suitable space — with every piece of training equipment included. Manikins, AED trainers, dressings, and all practical resources are brought to you. Certificates are issued on the day of training, and your business is audit-ready immediately.

We also cover first aid training in Rochester, first aid courses in Gillingham, and across the whole Medway area — useful if your business operates from multiple sites.
