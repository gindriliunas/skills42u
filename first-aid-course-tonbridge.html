About This Service

First aid training for Tonbridge's business community

Tonbridge sits at the heart of the Tonbridge and Malling district in west Kent, with a business mix spanning professional services and retail in the town centre, light industrial and logistics operations along the River Medway corridor, and one of Kent's largest out-of-town business campuses at Kings Hill. Whatever your sector, the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981 require you to have adequate first aid provision in place for your workforce.

Skills 42U comes to your Tonbridge premises — whether that's a meeting room in your Kings Hill office, a warehouse at Paddock Wood, or a nursery in Hildenborough — with every piece of training equipment included. Your team trains in familiar surroundings, certificates are issued on the day, and your business remains fully compliant from the moment the course ends.

We also serve first aid courses in Tunbridge Wells, first aid training in Maidstone, and across west and central Kent — ideal if your organisation operates across multiple locations.
